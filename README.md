# 🍫 Chocolate Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Chocolate Sales Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides comprehensive insights into sales performance, profitability, shipment trends, product performance, geographical revenue distribution, and salesperson performance.

The objective is to enable business stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify top-performing products and regions, and make data-driven business decisions.

## 🚀 Key Features

- Executive KPI Cards
  - Total Sales Amount
  - Total Boxes Sold
  - Total Shipments
  - Total Profit
  - Profit Percentage

- Time Intelligence Analysis
  - Annual Revenue (Current Year vs Previous Year)
  - Boxes Sold (Current Year vs Previous Year)

- Shipment Analysis
  - Shipment Distribution Histogram

- Product Insights
  - Top 6 Products by Sales
  - Product-wise Revenue
  - Product-wise Profit Margin

- Salesperson Performance
  - Sales Amount
  - Boxes Sold
  - Profit %

- Geographic Analysis
  - Revenue Distribution by Country

- Interactive Date Range Filter

## 📈 Business Insights

- Track overall business performance using executive KPIs.
- Compare current year performance with previous year.
- Identify top-selling and high-profit products.
- Evaluate salesperson performance based on revenue and profit.
- Analyze shipment volume distribution.
- Understand revenue contribution from different countries.
- Discover products with low or negative profit margins.
